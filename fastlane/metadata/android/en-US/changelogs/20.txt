* SynapMusic v1.0.0 Release!
* Added local music scanner to easily browse and play audio files on your device
* Fixed offline Vault playback issues with scanned local tracks
* High-res audio support: seamlessly transcode and play FLAC, WavPack, Opus, and AAC in Vault
* Reduced app download size down to ~24 MB by streamlining audio decoders
* Equalizer and DSP enhancements with 5-band tuning and 3D Surround
* Real-time synchronized lyrics with multi-language translation
* Backup and restore library with portable encrypted SynapCube (.synapcube) files
* Performance optimizations and playback reliability fixes
